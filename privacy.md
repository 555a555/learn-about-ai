
# Privacy Policy — Learn About AI

Last updated: October 20, 2026

## Overview

Learn About AI is an automated video creation project that generates AI-assisted videos and may help the account owner prepare content for sharing on TikTok.

## Information We Access

When you authorize the application to connect to TikTok, it may access basic profile information and permissions you explicitly approve. It may use video content, captions, and related information required to upload content to TikTok.

## How Information Is Used

Information is used only to operate the video creation and publishing workflow, provide the features requested by the account owner, and troubleshoot technical issues.

## Data Sharing and Storage

The project does not intentionally sell personal information. Video and audio content may be processed by third-party services used in the workflow, such as video rendering and text-to-speech providers. Those providers may process data according to their own privacy policies.

## Data Retention and Deletion

The account owner can stop using the workflow and revoke the application's access through TikTok settings. Requests to delete information associated with this project can be directed to the project owner.

## Security

Reasonable steps are taken to protect credentials and access tokens. Users should not share API keys, client secrets, or access tokens publicly.

## Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the owner of the Learn About AI project through the GitHub profile associated with this repository.
